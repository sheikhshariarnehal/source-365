# 3. Isolate Business Partnerships and Currency Exchange to Dedicated Route

## Context
Source 365 offers commercial opportunities including Business Partnerships, Digital Asset Sales, Subscriptions, and Currency Exchange (Dollar Buy & Sell). Displaying currency exchange directly on the agency homepage creates regulatory, compliance, and ad-network (Google Ads / Meta Ads) flagging risks.

## Decision
We decided to keep the homepage purely focused on core IT Services and the Growth Program, while placing Business Partnerships, Subscriptions, and Currency Exchange on a dedicated, compliant `/business` subpage with explicit legal and regulatory disclaimers.

## Consequences
- Protects the primary agency homepage from advertising platform flags or policy violations.
- Gives commercial partners and currency clients a dedicated, professional environment with requisite disclaimers.
