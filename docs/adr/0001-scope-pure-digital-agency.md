# 1. Focus on Pure B2B Agency and Growth Services (Excluding Skill Courses)

## Context
The initial agency draft (`source365-website-content.md`) included an educational skill development branch featuring eight online/offline training courses.

## Decision
We decided to completely exclude skill courses and training programs from the website, focusing 100% on B2B IT Services, the structured Growth Program, and commercial Business Solutions.

## Consequences
- All navigation, homepage sections, and route stubs related to courses, tutorials, and training academy features are removed.
- Brand messaging is unified around professional agency execution and measurable business scaling.
