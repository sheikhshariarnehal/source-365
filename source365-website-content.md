## 2. About Source 365

Source 365 is a digital solutions and skill development platform
offering IT services, professional training courses, and business
opportunities. Our goal is to help individuals, entrepreneurs, and
businesses grow through technology, digital marketing, and practical
skills.

We are building extraordinary digital experiences. Your ultimate
destination for A to Z IT services, highly demanding skill courses, and
business scaling solutions.

## 3. IT Services

### Digital Marketing (Boosting Service)

Professional Facebook post boosting and promotion services to help
businesses reach their targeted audiences effectively.

### Digital Marketing (Growth Program)

A structured digital marketing program that provides guidance for online
branding and marketing. The program includes a monthly content calendar,
competitor analysis, market research, marketing plans, funnel design,
content guidelines, campaign monitoring, daily updates, and performance
reports.

### Web Development

Professional website and web application development services for businesses, organizations, and individuals. Offerings include WordPress & WooCommerce websites, modern high-performance React & Next.js websites, and custom full-stack MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) web applications and dashboards.

### App Development

Mobile application development services tailored to business
requirements and user needs.

### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Search engine optimization services to improve website structure,
content, and visibility in search results.

### SQA (Software Quality Assurance)

Software testing and quality assurance services to help improve the
reliability, functionality, and usability of applications.

### Server Side Tracking

Server-side tracking setup and integration to support analytics, event
measurement, and advertising performance tracking.

### Graphics Design

Creative design services for branding, social media, advertising, and
other digital communication needs.
