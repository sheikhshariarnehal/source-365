# Source 365 Agency

Source 365 is a full-service digital solutions agency providing IT services, business scaling solutions, and end-to-end digital growth programs for businesses and entrepreneurs.

## Language

**IT Services**:
The technical, creative, and engineering solutions delivered directly to clients, including Web Development, App Development, SEO, SQA, Server-Side Tracking, and Graphics Design.
_Avoid_: Courses, training, tutorials, classes.

**Boosting Service**:
Targeted Facebook post promotion and audience reach optimization for businesses.
_Avoid_: Boost ads, generic marketing, social posts.

**Growth Program**:
A structured, end-to-end digital branding and marketing package combining content calendars, competitor analysis, funnel design, campaign monitoring, and scheduled performance reporting.
_Avoid_: Retainer, consultation package, marketing subscription.

**Key Account Manager**:
A dedicated growth professional assigned to each Growth Program client to manage campaigns, provide daily updates, and deliver 7/15/30-day performance reports.
_Avoid_: Support rep, ticket agent, account executive.

**Server-Side Tracking**:
Advanced analytics and conversion measurement infrastructure implemented server-side for high data precision and robust ad attribution.
_Avoid_: Pixel tracking, client-side tracking, cookie tracker.

**Business Solutions**:
Commercial opportunities and digital asset services provided by Source 365, including Business Partnerships, Digital Product Sales, Subscriptions, and Currency Exchange.
_Avoid_: Store, retail sales, marketplace.
